# Contributing to Awesome AI Resources

Thank you for helping make this one of the highest-quality AI resource directories on the web! Both this GitHub repository and the interactive edition at [aviperera.com/ai-resources/](https://aviperera.com/ai-resources/) aim to remain neutral, accurate, and useful to researchers, policymakers, engineers, and everyday practitioners.

## Guidelines

1. **Check for duplicates first**: Search [README.md](README.md) or [data/resources.json](data/resources.json) to ensure the resource is not already listed.
2. **Use canonical HTTPS URLs**: Submit official homepages or canonical documentation portals — avoid affiliate links, tracking parameters, or third-party SEO blog summaries.
3. **Keep descriptions neutral and concise**: One to two factual sentences explaining what the resource provides, who it serves, and free vs. paid access.
4. **Match category & tags**: Include the target category, pricing tag (`free`, `open-source`, `freemium`, or `paid`), and 1–3 focus facets (`policy`, `safety`, `academic`, `research`, `developer`, `productivity`, `creative`, `learning`).
5. **Explain why it belongs**: In your pull request or issue, briefly note what sets the resource apart (adoption, institutional backing, empirical rigour, or unique capability).
